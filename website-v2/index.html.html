01 / About

Marketing found me. I just couldn't stop watching it.

I didn't study marketing. I studied why one campaign works and an almost identical one doesn't. Why people trust one creator and scroll straight past another. That question is still the whole job.

Today I work as Talent Manager at ZInC, Zedital's talent management and influencer marketing division — sitting between creators and the brands trying to reach them, deciding who's actually worth the bet.

Brand, content, and talent aren't three separate interests to me. They're one obsession, examined from three angles.
